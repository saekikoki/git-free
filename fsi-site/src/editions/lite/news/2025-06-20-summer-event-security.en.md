title: Accepting Requests for Summer Event Security
summary: We are accepting requests for crowd control and traffic control security at summer events such as fireworks displays and festivals.
---
We are accepting requests for **crowd control** and **traffic control** security at summer events such as fireworks displays and festivals.

Large events require meetings with police and fire departments, so we recommend consulting us **two to three months before the event**.

- Security plan (free consultation)
- Site visit in advance and coordination with authorities
- Headquarters operation on the day and a report afterwards

See [Crowd Control Security](/en/services/crowd-security) for details.
