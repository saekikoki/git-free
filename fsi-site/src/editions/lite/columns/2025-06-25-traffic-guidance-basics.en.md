Traffic guidance security keeps pedestrians and vehicles safe around road works.

## When to apply

Contact us as soon as the schedule is set. We recommend **1–2 weeks ahead** so we can arrange staff.

## How many guards

It depends on road width, traffic volume and the work area. We propose a realistic number after a site visit.
