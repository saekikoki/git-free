---
title: Five Steps to a Successful Cloud Migration
summary: How to move from on-premises to clouds such as AWS without runaway costs or business downtime, explained in five steps.
---
"We moved to the cloud to save money, but our monthly bill went up." We hear this a lot.
This article introduces five steps for a successful cloud migration.

## 1. Take inventory of your current systems

Start by listing the servers and systems you run today. Commonly overlooked items include:

- Batch jobs that run only at night
- Data integrations with other systems
- Small tools running on staff PCs

## 2. Decide the migration approach

For each system, decide whether to move it as is or rebuild it.

| Approach | What it means | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Rehost | Move servers to the cloud almost as they are | Systems near end of support |
| Replatform | Replace parts such as databases with managed services | Reducing operational effort |
| Rebuild | Re-architect for the cloud | Long-lived systems with frequent changes |

## 3. Estimate costs

Include data transfer and backup storage, not just server costs.

## 4. Start small, then migrate production

Migrate low-impact systems first to validate procedures and performance. Always rehearse before the production cutover.

## 5. Keep reviewing costs after migration

Migration is not the end. Right-sizing servers to actual usage keeps lowering your costs.
